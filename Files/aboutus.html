About Tropicart: Our Core Mission

We are dedicated to bringing you the freshest, most vibrant flavors with an unwavering commitment to health and the planet.

Our Journey

The Idea That Started
With a Simple Goal

Elaine and Miky have always shared a simple belief: the best flavors come from nature itself. What started as a small curiosity—mixing fruits, herbs, and locally found ingredients in their own kitchen—slowly grew into a passion they couldn’t ignore. They noticed how refreshing it felt to enjoy drinks made without artificial colors, heavy sugars, or preservatives. Just real ingredients with real flavor..

Our Promise

Bottled with Love,
Served with Freshness

We believe that what you put into your body matters. That's why we source our ingredients from local, organic farms that prioritize sustainable practices. No preservatives, no concentrates—just pure, liquid nature.

Our Vision

Sustainable Choices for a
Healthier Future

Tropicart is committed to minimizing our environmental impact. From compostable packaging to zero-waste production methods, every choice we make supports a cleaner planet.

  • Eco-friendly and recyclable packaging
  • Direct sourcing from ethical, local farms
  • 100% plant-based and cruelty-free
  • Supporting community health initiatives
Impact & Community

More Than Just Juice:
Giving Back to the Planet

For every bottle of Tropicart sold, we plant one tree through our partnership with 'Green Earth Now.' We also donate 1% of our profits monthly to local food security programs, ensuring our freshest ingredients reach those who need them most.
